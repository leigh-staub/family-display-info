Privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Family Display is a private wall calendar used by one household. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With permission, it reads the household's Google Calendar using read-only access. It cannot create, change or delete events.

What it keeps

It stores no copy of calendar data. Events are read live each time the calendar is shown and are not saved.

Sharing

Calendar data is never sold or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
